Reef sharks, including whitetip and blacktip reef sharks, patrol the deep outer reef wall.

Pelagic coral structures grow on the steep drop-off of the reef wall.

Underwater currents on the reef wall can be strong - divers should be experienced and careful.

Manta rays are frequently spotted along the outer reef walls during plankton blooms.

Giant groupers can grow over 2 meters long and are found near the reef wall drop-offs.

The deep wall drops to over 50 meters, creating a habitat for large pelagic species.

Visiting divers must use a depth gauge to avoid decompression sickness.

The wall is home to schools of barracuda, tuna, and trevally.

Deep wall dives require advanced certification and proper equipment.
